Family Hub has an **Accessibility** section in the calendar's Settings
(2026.11.1.16+) for anyone in the family who finds the colors, the text or the
buttons hard to use. It works on every Family Hub card, not just the calendar:
Today, Right Now, Chores and My Chores, Rewards, Goals, To-Do Lists, My Pantry
and the Recipe Box all follow it.

<img src="/HA-Family-Hub/img/screenshots/settings-accessibility.webp" alt="The Accessibility section: Applies to, Color vision, the colors-that-look-alike check, High contrast, Text size, Font, Reduce motion and Bigger buttons" width="460" />

## Whole household or just this device

**Applies to** decides where your choices go:

- **Whole household** saves them with the household settings, so every
  device and every card uses them. Choose this when the person who needs it
  uses all the screens.
- **Just this device** keeps them on this screen only: Grandpa's tablet gets
  big text, the kitchen display stays as it is. A device with its own choices
  keeps them when the household ones change. Switch back to **Whole
  household** and save to make it follow the household again.

An admin can also set them for any device from the
[Devices tab](/docs/settings-modal#devices-tab-admin-only).

## Color vision

About 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women see some colors as alike, most often red
and green. With **Shapes & patterns** on, each person also gets a shape (on
their avatar and dots) and a pattern (a strip down the side of their events
and name pills), drawn in a darker shade of their own color. Their color stays,
so nothing changes for anyone who can see it, but the calendar can now be read
without it.

<img src="/HA-Family-Hub/img/screenshots/a11y-calendar-shapes.webp" alt="The week with Shapes & patterns on: each person's events have their own pattern strip and shape" />

The shapes and patterns are the same on every card, in the household's order:
Mom's circle and solid strip on the calendar is her circle and solid strip on
Today, Right Now and Chores too.

<img src="/HA-Family-Hub/img/screenshots/a11y-today-shapes.webp" alt="The Today card with each person's shape instead of a plain dot" width="560" />

<img src="/HA-Family-Hub/img/screenshots/a11y-right-now-shapes.webp" alt="Right Now tiles with each person's pattern strip and avatar shape" />

The event happening now is outlined and marked **NOW** in capitals, so it no
longer depends on the green.

### Colors that look alike

Under Color vision, Family Hub checks the people's colors against the three
common kinds of color blindness. If two people's colors would look almost the
same to someone (Mom's pink and Leo's green, say), it says so and suggests a
new color for as few people as it can. **Use it for Leo** puts the color in
Leo's row under People; tap **Save** to keep it. The check shows even with
Shapes & patterns off, because a better color helps everyone.

## High contrast

Black text on white, strong outlines and no see-through panels. It's the same
look as the Black & white [E-ink screen](./eink-screens) mode, on a normal
screen: each person's pattern shows on their events, but the calendar still
redraws every minute and the edit pencils stay.

<img src="/HA-Family-Hub/img/screenshots/a11y-calendar-high-contrast.webp" alt="The week in High contrast: black on white with a pattern per person" />

## Text size

From 100% to 200%. It makes everything on the cards bigger at once, buttons
included. Long words wrap with a hyphen where they can. On a wide week with all
seven days, 150% and up gets tight; the Day or Agenda view suits big text
best.

<img src="/HA-Family-Hub/img/screenshots/a11y-calendar-text-150.webp" alt="The week at 150% text size" />

## Font

- **Theme font** uses whatever your theme uses.
- **Easy to read** is Atkinson Hyperlegible, made by the Braille Institute for
  low vision: letters like I, l and 1 look clearly different.
- **Dyslexia-friendly** is Lexend, with a little more space between letters
  and words.

Both fonts come with Family Hub, so they work without the internet.

<img src="/HA-Family-Hub/img/screenshots/a11y-calendar-dyslexia.webp" alt="The week in the Dyslexia-friendly font" />

## Reduce motion

No confetti, animations or sliding panels. **Follow this device** (the
default) uses the phone or tablet's own Reduce motion setting. **Always**
turns them off everywhere, and **Off** keeps them even on a device set to
reduce motion. On the screen saver, widgets still move a little every few
minutes to protect the screen from burn-in, but they jump instead of gliding.

## Bigger buttons

Larger buttons, checkboxes and + button, easier for small fingers or
unsteady hands.
